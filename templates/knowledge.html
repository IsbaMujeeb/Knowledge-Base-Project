Equipment Structural Hierarchy

Shaft Bearings

Support radial and axial shaft rotor dynamics with precision tolerances.

Failure Modes: Raceway spalling, cage fracture, dry lubrication Critical Sensor: Vibration velocity > 6.5 mm/s, Temp > 75°C

Closed Impeller

Imparts centrifugal velocity to liquid inside the casing volute chamber.

Failure Modes: Micro-jet cavitation pitting, vane erosion, debris clogging Critical Indicator: Popping acoustics, suction head drop

Mechanical Seal

Provides fluid containment barrier preventing hazardous fluid egress along the shaft.

Failure Modes: Carbon face cracking, secondary O-ring hardening Critical Indicator: Continuous fluid leakage, gland temperature

Flexible Coupling

Transmits mechanical motor torque to the pump shaft while absorbing misalignment.

Failure Modes: Angular misalignment, elastomeric spider shearing Critical Sensor: Elevated 1X/2X rotational frequency vibration
